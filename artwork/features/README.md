## Final prompt set

Each prompt consists of the following shared prefix plus its scene paragraph.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create one premium cinematic game marketing illustration for a landscape 3:2 website feature card for Dino Warfront, a prehistoric strategy game. Detailed painterly realism, dark jungle greens, warm amber sunlight, weathered leather, bronze and stone, atmospheric depth, crisp readable silhouettes at small card size. Full-bleed environmental scene, focal subjects in central 80 percent for responsive cropping. No text, no letters, no UI, no frame, no icon, no badge, no watermark. Illustrative artwork, not a fake gameplay screenshot.

### Alliance

> Scene: three distinct human tribal commanders in practical leather and bronze armor gather around a large stone campaign table at a fortified jungle outpost. They cooperate over an unlettered terrain map with small markers. Behind them stand allied warriors with simple plain banners, a watchtower and a saddled dinosaur. Emphasize teamwork, shared planning and a believable prehistoric alliance. Medium-wide composition, faces and table readable, no combat or wounds.

### Army

> Scene: a disciplined prehistoric army trains in a spacious fortified settlement courtyard. In the foreground a clear small squad of spear-and-shield infantry practices formation; behind and to one side archers practice with bows toward wooden targets, and to the other side armored riders train on sturdy raptor-like dinosaurs. Distinct roles in one coherent environment. Wide ground-level composition, restrained number of figures with sound anatomy, practical leather and bronze equipment, no futuristic vehicles or rifles, no combat wounds.

### Dinosaurs

> Scene: a majestic Tyrannosaurus stands in a lush prehistoric jungle clearing, a Triceratops walks in the midground and two smaller Velociraptors explore the foreground beside ferns. Dramatic cliffs, distant waterfall and warm mist, varied natural scale patterns in earthy colors, realistic dinosaur anatomy. Medium-wide nature tableau with full animals and ample environment, not cropped head portraits. No people, no armor, no dragons, no battle or wounds.
